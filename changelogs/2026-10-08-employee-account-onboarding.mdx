Start employee onboarding and manage account access from Employees.

- See employment status, account access and onboarding progress separately.
- Link eligible existing accounts without replacing their permissions.
- HR can activate standard employee accounts and request secure password setup emails.
- Super Admin controls privileged account activation and project management access roles.
- Email setup requests clearly distinguish a request from confirmed delivery.
