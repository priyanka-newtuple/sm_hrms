---
title: "UAT email inbox inside HRMS"
date: "2026-10-26"
tags: ["Feature"]
description: "HR testers can inspect captured emails using their existing HRMS login."
---

When enabled for UAT, HR Cockpit includes an email inbox for authorized HR Full and Super Admin testers.

- Search captured emails, check recipients, preview messages and download attachments.
- Use existing HRMS login and HTTPS; no terminal commands or separate mailbox password.
- Captured messages are not delivered to real recipients. Workflow actions still require the intended test account.
- Deployment can disable the inbox with `HRMS_UAT_INBOX_ENABLED=false`. Live email mode always disables it.
- Native notifications and Mailpit storage remain unchanged; no public Mailpit ports or relay actions are added.
