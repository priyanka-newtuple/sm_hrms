date: "2026-07-30"

tags: ["Feature", "Security"]
---

Two new access-control layers give admins finer control over what roles can see and do.

- **Workflow-level access by role** — assign roles to specific workflows so users only see and interact with the workflows they're permitted to access
- **Field-level RBAC on dashboards** — dashboard queries and metric endpoints now respect field-level permissions, ensuring sensitive fields are never exposed in widgets or charts to users who lack permission
- **Permission sync on workflow config** — permissions and entity lists are automatically resynced after configuring a workflow, preventing stale access rules
